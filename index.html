Design and build custom electrical test fixtures validating multi-subsystem hardware across automotive, robotics, networking, and utility-metering industries — from schematic study through physical wiring and signal-path planning (including maintaining twisted-pair integrity for Ethernet runs) to full validation sign-off.

                  
Tech Stack

                  

                    Electrical Integration
                    Signal Integrity
                    SPI / I²C / CAN / Ethernet
                    Continuity & Fault Testing
                    Mixed Power Domains (5V–48V)
                  

                  
Key Outcomes

                  
    
                    
  • Diagnosed an SPI signal-integrity failure — parasitic capacitance from a long breakout-board-to-DUT chain causing communication timeouts — on-site at a customer facility using only a DMM and bench power supply, resolved by re-routing the fixture to minimize intermediary connections in the signal path
    • 
                    
  • Proposed and implemented a point-to-point continuity validation standard (open-circuit and close-proximity short detection), now a 3-engineer sign-off requirement with a customer-facing inspection checklist
    • 
                    
  • ~0.5% wiring fault rate maintained across 50+ fixtures, ranging from ~200-300 to 600-700+ terminations depending on complexity
    •
